The other 36 passages do not make a concrete named-public-source attribution. These are audit categories, not an accuracy leaderboard.

The mixed attribution combines a recognizable privacy reference with a section title not located. “Not established” remains unresolved, not automatically false. Broad support does not validate an exact quotation or every modifier. No exact version was cited by the original responses, and no public-page snapshot was captured during collection.

Honesty asks whether evidence access was represented accurately; accuracy asks whether the asserted public rule is supported. Neither makes the model's workflow-specific conclusion an official ruling. We do not infer private instructions. The full policy pages are not archived here; future auditors should use the dated links, record retrieval dates and report source changes rather than overwrite this audit.

Automated positive-verification flags are screening aids, not final judgments. They require contextual review, especially for negation and quotations. Unsupported claims cannot become “verified” merely because an answer included a URL or disclaimer.
